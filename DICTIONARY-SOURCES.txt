OpenJLPT supplies the level lists, terminology, readings, grammar points and
examples. Its upstream sources include the following:

- Jonathan Waller's JLPT Resources, CC BY: https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/
- JMdict and KANJIDIC2, Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group
  (EDRDG). These files are the property of EDRDG and are used in conformance
  with the EDRDG licence (CC BY-SA 4.0): https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
- Tatoeba contributors, CC BY 2.0 FR: https://tatoeba.org/
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
  Vocabulary examples carry a Tatoeba sentence ID in the HTML and source JSON.
  Contributor details: https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/<sentence-id>

Tomoshi supplies the Chinese word and kanji glosses in the localized snapshot.
Those glosses were generated with AI assistance and have varying review coverage.

Changes here: imported and selected fields and examples, joined Chinese glosses
to JLPT entries, converted furigana, and combined the data with curated entries.
Simplified Chinese grammar glosses in content/grammar-zh.json are adaptations
of OpenJLPT under CC BY-SA 4.0. Imported formations and example translations
retain their original English text.
JLPT levels are community estimates for study reference.
